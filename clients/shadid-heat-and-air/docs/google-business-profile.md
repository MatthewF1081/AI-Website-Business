# Google Business Profile setup — Shadid Heat and Air

The current Google listing has 4.8★ from 24 reviews at 4907 NW 23rd St, Oklahoma City, OK 73127, phone +1 866-227-8752. It shows **"Claim this business"** and **"Add website"**, so it is **unclaimed** and has no website linked. Fixing that is the single biggest local SEO win.

## 1. Claim and verify (do first)
1. Sign in with the business Google account. Use the owner's account, and add yourself as a Manager, not the Owner.
2. Open the listing in Google Maps → **Claim this business** → **Manage now**.
3. Verify the business. Google picks the method: a video recording, phone, text or postcard. For a video:
   - Show the street sign or the outside of the shop.
   - Show the business signage or a branded vehicle.
   - Show proof of management, like unlocking the door, tools in use, or a business document.

## 2. Fix the business name ⚠️
The listing is currently named **"Heating & AC Air Conditioning Oklahoma City."** Keyword-stuffed names break Google's guidelines and are a common cause of **profile suspension**. Rename it to the real business name, exactly as it appears on signage and the website: **Shadid Heat and Air**. The name, address and phone (NAP) must match the website footer character-for-character.

## 6. Reviews
- Turn on review replies and **reply to every review** within a few days. Mention the service and the city naturally, e.g. "Thanks for trusting us with your furnace replacement in Bethany!"
- Get the **review link**: Profile → *Ask for reviews*. Put the Place ID in `googlePlaceId` in `build.js`.
- Text the review link after every completed job (most CRMs can automate this).
- Never offer incentives for reviews or post reviews on someone's behalf.

## 7. Ongoing (monthly)
- One or two **Google Posts** a month: seasonal tune-up reminders, recent jobs, offers.
- Answer and seed **Q&A**, using the website FAQs.
- Keep hours, holiday hours and services up to date.
- Check **Performance** for calls, website clicks and direction requests.
