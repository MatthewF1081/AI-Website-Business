FAQ

Common questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve the whole Oklahoma City metro from our shop on NW 23rd St, including Bethany, Warr Acres, Yukon, Mustang, Edmond, The Village, Nichols Hills, Del City, Midwest City, Moore and Piedmont.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Estimates for new systems and ductwork are free. For repairs we charge a flat diagnostic fee and tell you the exact repair price before we do any work.

Can I text you instead of calling?

Absolutely. Tap the Text button on any page and send us a quick message — many of our customers prefer it.

What brands do you work on?

We service and repair nearly every brand of furnace, air conditioner and heat pump, including Trane, Carrier, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem and American Standard.
