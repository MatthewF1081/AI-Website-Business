Warning signs

Do you need furnace & heating?

If you notice any of these, it's time to call. Catching problems early almost always costs less.

  • Rising utility bills
  • Some rooms much colder than others

FAQ

Furnace & Heating questions

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the house, don't flip any switches, and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Once it's safe, call us and we'll find and fix the source.

Do you work on heat pumps?

Yes. We repair and replace air-source heat pumps and dual-fuel systems, which are common across the OKC metro.

How often should my furnace be serviced?

Once a year, ideally in the fall. A tune-up catches worn parts before the first cold night and keeps your warranty valid.
